## 🧪 What Happens Behind the Scenes

The tool performs three actions safely:

1. **Clears Scratch Disk Cache** - Deletes temporary files in the folder Photoshop uses for scratch space. These files are safe to remove.

2. **Resets Memory Settings** - Changes Photoshop's preference file back to default values. This fixes cases where Photoshop gave itself too little or too much RAM.

3. **Restores Default Performance Options** - Removes custom memory sliders and cache levels that might be causing conflicts.

The tool does not touch your actual image files. It does not delete your brushes, presets, actions, or any other personal settings.

## 🔁 If the Error Continues After Running the Tool

The tool fixes most cases. But sometimes the problem comes from outside Photoshop. Here is what to check if the error remains.

### Check Your Free Disk Space

1. Open File Explorer
2. Right-click on your C: drive (the drive where Windows is installed)
3. Select "Properties"
4. Look at "Free Space"

You need at least 10 GB free. Photoshop uses your main hard drive for scratch space. If you have less than 10 GB, free up space by:

- Moving large video files to an external drive
- Uninstalling programs you do not use
- Emptying the Recycle Bin

### Adjust Photoshop's Scratch Disk Assignment

Photoshop lets you choose which drives to use. Sometimes it picks a nearly-full drive.

1. Open Photoshop
2. Go to **Edit** → **Preferences** → **Scratch Disks**
3. Uncheck your C: drive
4. Check any other drives you have (D:, E:, etc.)
5. Click OK
6. Restart Photoshop

The tool makes this change easier by resetting to defaults. But if you have multiple drives, you should assign Photoshop to the one with the most free space.

### Restart Your Computer

Temporary files sometimes stay locked by other programs. A simple restart clears these locks.

1. Close Photoshop
2. Restart Windows
3. Open Photoshop again

### Check for Photoshop Updates

Adobe releases fixes regularly.

1. Open Creative Cloud app
2. Go to "Updates"
3. Click "Update" next to Photoshop
